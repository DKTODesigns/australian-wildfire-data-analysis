# australian-wildfire-data-analysis
🌏 Australian Wildfire Activity Analysis
📌 Project Overview

Wildfires are a recurring and increasingly severe environmental challenge in Australia. Understanding when, where, and why wildfire activity intensifies is critical for both environmental monitoring and risk mitigation.

In this project, I explored historical wildfire data to uncover patterns in:

🔥 Fire intensity and brightness
📍 Regional wildfire distribution
📅 Seasonal and yearly trends
📊 Relationships between fire characteristics

Using Python-based data visualization techniques, I transformed raw wildfire data into clear, actionable insights.

🧰 Tools & Technologies
Python
Pandas (data manipulation)
Matplotlib & Seaborn (data visualization)
Folium (geospatial mapping)
Google Colab (development environment)
📊 Key Insights & Visual Analysis
🔥 Fire Activity Trends Over Time

Wildfire activity fluctuates significantly year over year, with noticeable spikes indicating periods of intensified fire activity. These peaks may correlate with environmental conditions such as drought or extreme heat.

📅 Seasonal Patterns in Wildfires

Wildfires show strong seasonal behavior, with certain months consistently experiencing higher fire activity. This highlights the importance of seasonal preparedness and monitoring.

🌍 Regional Fire Distribution

A large proportion of wildfire activity is concentrated in specific regions:

Northern Territory (NT)
Western Australia (WA)
Queensland (QL)

This suggests geographic and climatic influences play a major role in wildfire frequency.

💡 Fire Brightness by Region

While wildfire frequency varies across regions, the intensity (brightness) remains relatively consistent, indicating similar fire behavior once ignition occurs.

📉 Distribution of Fire Brightness

The distribution shows that most fires fall within a consistent brightness range, with fewer extreme outliers. This helps identify typical vs abnormal fire events.

🔗 Relationship Between Radiative Power and Confidence

The scatter plot reveals:

High confidence detections cluster across a wide range of radiative power
Weak correlation between power and confidence

This suggests that confidence levels are influenced by more than just fire intensity.

📍 Geographic Mapping of Wildfire Regions

Using Folium, wildfire regions were mapped geographically to provide spatial context. This visualization reinforces how wildfire activity is distributed across Australia’s landscape.

🧠 Key Takeaways
Wildfire activity is highly seasonal and region-dependent
Certain regions consistently contribute the majority of fire activity
Fire intensity is relatively stable across regions
Data visualization is essential for uncovering environmental patterns
Combining statistical and geographic views provides deeper insight
